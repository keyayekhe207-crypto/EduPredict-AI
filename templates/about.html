About EduPredict AI

What this tool does, how it works, and where its limits are.

What is this?

EduPredict AI is a local decision-support tool for teachers of 12th-standard students. It analyses attendance, marks, study habits and other academic signals to flag students who may benefit from extra attention, well before final results.

It runs entirely on your own computer using CSV files — there is no database and no data ever leaves your machine.

How the prediction works

A Random Forest classifier (a machine learning model made of many decision trees voting together) is trained on data/students.csv using ten academic features: attendance, previous exam percentage, internal marks, assignment marks, practical marks, study hours, class participation, homework completion, previous backlogs, and previous test score.

For each student, the model outputs one of three categories — At Risk, Average, or Good — along with a confidence level. A separate, transparent rule-based risk score (0–100) is calculated from the same fields so a teacher can see exactly which factors are driving the result.

Strengths, concerns and recommendations are generated per student from their own specific numbers — no two students receive identical advice unless their data genuinely matches.

Important limitations

  • This is an educational decision-support prototype, not a scientifically validated academic or psychological assessment tool.
  • Predictions are based on a small sample dataset and simple features — they can be wrong, and should never be the sole basis for a decision about a student.
  • The teacher always remains responsible for interpreting results and deciding what action, if any, to take.
  • Login is a local demo system for coursework purposes and is not intended for production or sensitive real student data.
